## Full research idea and contribution boundary

The question is whether a model-switching agent can improve terminal task success or resource use by choosing a *versioned model configuration* at each eligible decision using observed history, and whether randomized logs can estimate its value with uncertainty calibrated against fresh executions. The intervention fixes the model pool, decoding, prompt format, tools, execution harness, stopping and outcome rules; generated text and subsequent tool states evolve after a switch. The target policy value is `V(π)=E[Y^π]`. The fixed-benchmark contrast is separate from an inference target over newly sampled tasks. Routing-probability ratios identify policy values only under consistency, sequential exchangeability, support and stable execution kernels. A fitted outcome model cannot rescue unsupported actions or a changed harness.

The proposed contribution is a precise intervention/measurement contract and an **empirically validated evaluation design**, not a newly invented DTR, IPW, doubly robust estimator or generic optimality theorem. Read the [research proposal](research_proposal.md), [theory/proofs](theory.md), [claim map](paper_positioning.md), [literature/code review](literature_design_review_20260921.md) and [v2 protocol](experiment_protocol_v2.md). The editable [LaTeX manuscript](../manuscript/main.tex) and [PDF](../manuscript/DTR_Agent_Regimes_Theory_Draft.pdf) are a 38-page working draft, **not** a submitted preprint.

The fixed primary empirical target is a fresh, source-bound **SWE-bench Verified repository-repair** contrast of history-aware versus matched prompt-only routing, with the same initial action and learner/objective restrictions, competent executor pair, consequential second-decision opportunity, strict evaluator endpoint, resource accounting and valid uncertainty for the declared task/frame. Development controls and the older MBPP/HumanEval coding archive cannot be relabeled as confirmation. Compare live continuations rather than holding the switched model's downstream state fixed. The [v2 protocol](experiment_protocol_v2.md) governs task qualification, disjoint TRAIN/development/CONFIRM sets, source pins, support, precision and joint success/resource rules. No new live/CONFIRM stage or outcome-driven policy/metric tuning is released.

## Prioritized roadmap with acceptance criteria

**Verdict: HOLD a new live/CONFIRM routing stage; no new worker batch is queued.** This targeted hold is not a stop order for separately authorized work. Another synthetic null or routine infrastructure sweep would not answer the primary question. The lead must own target, comparator, utility, learning class and inference choices rather than delegate scientific judgment to the implementer.

1. **P0 — Make the fixed target executable.** Identify an actually accessible no-cost host and source-pinned executor pair with **independent, comparable repository-repair competence evidence**, or narrow the paper's empirical claim explicitly. Recheck host margin, sandbox/evaluator, licenses, environment and peer ownership on that host. Before any model batch, require a prespecified task/frame and exposure exclusions, strict no-change/reference controls, verified competence under comparable budgets, meaningful feedback-dependent second decision, source/config hashes and bounded resources. If these fail, record **BLOCKED**; do not present a weaker comparator as the intended study. REQ-019/020/021 are historical diagnostics, not permission to purchase or launch compute.
2. **P0 — Freeze comparison and inference before CONFIRM.** Match the initial action and learner/objective class for history-aware and prompt-only routers; include useful fixed/initial-only comparators and separate success/resource endpoints. Specify fixed-benchmark versus new-task estimand, task/family/period clustering, nuisance training/splits, support, multiplicity, precision and joint success/resource decision rule. Validate intervals in a matching simulator. Acceptance: immutable reviewed design/manifest and adequate decision opportunities/precision; no tuning on CONFIRM. REQ-018 and the null control are *retrospective diagnosis*, not prospective validation.
3. **P1 — Complete evidence-backed paper and reproducibility.** After eligible fresh evidence exists, audit raw-to-publication provenance and independently rerun critical analyses. Integrate favorable and unfavorable findings, limitations, figures/tables and consistent claims into LaTeX/PDF. Resolve author-approved title/authors/affiliations, licenses/attribution, final source/supplement and preprint requirements. Acceptance: independent reproduction or explicit irreproducible limits, clean build/visual review, metadata approval and author-ready package. No paper upload/submission has occurred.

For weak findings distinguish **estimand mismatch**, insufficient decision opportunities or power, unsuitable comparator/utility, learner restriction, implementation/inference defect, and true theory limitation or empirical null. Start with bounded deterministic retrospective checks; record evidence for and against each explanation, what remains unknown and a discriminating next check. See the [lead diagnosis](scientific_diagnosis_20260920.md) and dated [feedback files](experiment_handoff.md). Never use a favorable development slice to redefine a confirmatory endpoint.
