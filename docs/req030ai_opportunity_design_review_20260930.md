## Question and limits

Conditional on every AH task's unchanged/reference controls and source contract
being accepted, the audit asks whether a common pinned HF/BF16 executor pair has
strict repair successes, genuine second-decision opportunities and differing
responses worth studying. Common model/runtime, prompt, tokenizer, decoding,
context, tool, stopping, grading and budget contracts must be fixed before any
model episode. HF/BF16 is a separate treatment from the closed local GGUF path.

The [v2 protocol](experiment_protocol_v2.md#3-substantive-agent-study-two-decisions-inside-repository-repair)
places choices before logical calls 1 and 9, retaining the chosen backend for
calls 1–8 and 9–24. An absorbed episode remains in its assigned denominator.
Call 9 is eligible only when the episode is active and **both** backends satisfy
the common pre-action reservation rule. Record that decision before invocation;
never create an opportunity using a hidden grade, redraw an action on retry or
use an unlogged fallback.

SS/SL supply **16 assigned S-start trajectories**, the relevant opportunity
denominator for the primary matched-initial H/P comparison. Report their call-9
eligibility separately from LS/LL and all 32 cells. Aggregate occupancy cannot
establish adequate opportunity after S. Distinguish public tool feedback from
terminal evaluation; a nonzero shell status is not automatically a test failure.

Four fresh schedules can show descriptive per-task discordance and feedback
variation. They do not establish a feedback-conditioned causal action effect,
executor superiority, a new-task population success rate or an H/P advantage.
Separately executed SS and SL prefixes are not counterfactual replays. Equal
greedy settings or seed integers do not prove a coupling. Pre-call-9 message and
workspace-state hashes may document actual agreement without assuming it.

Prospectively balance and interleave schedule order to limit execution-period
confounding. A candidate is a task-index Latin rotation, placing each of four
schedules in each position twice across eight tasks. The actual order and tie
rule remain unfrozen here. Preserve execution periods; interleaving alone does
not prove independence.

## Denominators and investment interpretation

The [existing feasibility flags](experiment_protocol_v2.md#5-endpoints-adequacy-and-resource-gates)
are **zero successes across all schedules, more than 10% infrastructure failures,
or fewer than 50% reaching decision 2**. Applied descriptively to 32 assigned
episodes these mean zero successes, at least four infrastructure failures, or
fewer than 16 eligible second decisions. The relevant S-start diagnostic is
separately fewer than eight of its 16 assigned trajectories. These are redesign
flags, not significance tests, task-exclusion rules or proof of a null effect.

For each eight-cell schedule report verified resolutions r, known unresolved
cells f and algorithmically unknown cells u, with r+f+u=8. The operational v2
resolution rate is r/8, retaining failures/missing verification in the denominator.
The corresponding unresolved-correctness sensitivity range is [r/8,(r+u)/8].
Report [R/32,(R+U)/32] for the complete cohort as a separate descriptive range.
These are bounds from missing outcome assignments, **not confidence intervals**,
population estimates or bounds on repeated-execution expected values. Preserve
unknown reasons and distinguish unstarted, infrastructure and evaluation failures.
Observed required SKIPPED/XFAIL statuses determine strict nonresolution but do not
by themselves prove algorithmic incorrectness. Aggregate the separate correctness
field; do not equate a completed strict-endpoint grade with known correctness.
Any evaluator retry allowance must be fixed prospectively and must never
regenerate a candidate using grading feedback.

The old AG rule requiring 2–6 successes in *at least one* model arm does not
establish a competent pair: it permits one executor to have zero successes.
Report SS and LL separately and judge whether both provide a usable comparator.
Possible numerical spending floors, including two successes per executor, remain
**proposed and unadopted**; none is statistical evidence of competence. Any such
rule must be justified and frozen before outcomes, not selected after this audit.

The decision is whether to fund one separately capped design/precision stage,
not whether a favorable H/P effect has been demonstrated. Infrastructure or
source invalidity holds model release. A completed audit with no useful
opportunity or executor discrimination supports stopping expansion of this
configuration and reporting its limitation; n=8 cannot establish that history is
universally useless. A positive feasibility pattern permits further design work,
not unrestricted compute or automatic CONFIRM. Do not search successive cohorts
until a favorable pattern appears.

## Resource envelope and unclosed gates

At 24 logical calls per cell, the 32-cell proposal permits at most **768 logical
calls**, **1,179,648 returned tokens** at 1,536 per call and **11,403,264 input
tokens** at the 14,848-token per-call reservation. These are logical-call bounds;
the physical-attempt/retry contract must also be explicit. Tokens are not dollars
or energy.

The component ceilings imply 600×32 seconds loading + 2,700×32 seconds per
episode + 900×32 seconds grading = **134,400 seconds = 37 hours 20 minutes**,
before additional setup/cleanup or permitted grading retries. The old 36-hour
allocation cannot promise completion of every component's worst case. A future
release must either specify an adequate bounded allocation or enforce a joint
cap and preserve every unstarted/interrupted cell as such; it must not silently
increase limits or drop cells. Actual timing measurements may improve planning
but do not change the declared ceiling by themselves.

Before model release, close AH all-task controls and source-tree review, the
integrated two-stage worker/native-call/sandbox lifetime gates, complete scoring
and receipt accounting, physical retry limits, balanced execution order, exact
source/model/image pins and the full resource envelope. Infrastructure tests or
CPU controls alone do not establish model competence.

Before the primary H/P study, additionally freeze trained H and P with initial S,
the same learner class/objective and preregistered features; a development-selected
strong fixed comparator; family-safe training/calibration/untouched evaluation;
actual logging probabilities; independent fresh policy references; and the
contrast family, numerical useful margin, covariance/independence contract and
calibrated uncertainty method. See [v2 comparators and sealed evaluation](experiment_protocol_v2.md#4-comparators-training-and-sealed-evaluation)
and the [precision audit](theory_precision_design_20260926.md). One execution per
task/schedule does not estimate within-task execution variance, and different
task IDs do not eliminate shared server/period dependence. No power calculation
is inferred from this purposive eight-task audit.
