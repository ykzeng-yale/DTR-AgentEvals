## Scientific interpretation corrections

1. **Only three live-evaluated policies satisfy the written primary utility rule.** `always_large`, `learned`, and `soft_escalation_d2` have a positive Bonferroni OPE contrast versus `always_small` and a positive fresh-live contrast. `large_then_small` and `soft_escalation_d4` have OPE estimates but no live arm; their live-sign requirement is unassessed. They must not be included in the combined-rule claim. `class_tailored` and `escalate_after_first_failure` do not pass its OPE requirement.
2. **Calibration evidence is descriptive and has uncertainty.** Five of six nominal 95% paired utility intervals include zero. That neither demonstrates equivalence nor estimates repeated-sampling coverage. `class_tailored` has a nominal discrepancy of approximately −0.037 (95% interval −0.070 to −0.005); it is a diagnostic to investigate, not proof of systematic bias or of a particular sparse-cell mechanism. Six pointwise comparisons do not supply a simultaneous calibration guarantee. The ranking correlation is descriptive, not a causal calibration test.
3. **The learned-versus-large comparison did not establish superiority or equal quality.** The secondary live utility difference is about −0.005 (95% interval −0.027 to +0.017); the success difference is about −0.008 (−0.029 to +0.014). Both permit effects of either sign. There is no prespecified equivalence or noninferiority margin. The roughly 11% lower observed large-call count (762 versus 858) is a separate resource description; total calls are higher (880 versus 858). A line connecting policy-level success and call means is not a causal dose-response curve.
4. **Separate evaluation-only from total collection cost.** The reported 3,662 calls are the CONFIRM portion of the randomized log; the complete log cost 6,063 calls, including 2,401 TRAIN calls. Fresh live comparisons cost 5,504 calls. Thus 3,662 versus 5,504 compares evaluation subsets (about 33% fewer calls). Charging the common frozen-router training to both pathways instead gives 6,063 versus 7,905 calls (about 23% fewer); charging it only to the log side would be asymmetric. Neither comparison alone is a complete end-to-end or equal-precision accounting. Report development, test generation, router training, fitting and branch costs, specify which are common/sunk, and keep calls, tokens, time and money separate. Only six of the nine offline targets have live evaluation. Higher weight ESS alone does not establish a cost-efficiency theorem for different policy targets.
5. **The published Theorem 5 calculation is hypothetical for these scores.** Its arithmetic is unchanged, but its external-fit independence assumption still does not hold for the reported cross-fitted CONFIRM construction. Preserve the calculation as a scale illustration; do not describe it as a valid certificate that is merely vacuous. A variance-adaptive bound is separate research and would not repair the present applicability issue by itself.

The utility rule is primary; success comparisons are secondary. Do not imply that the utility-family multiplicity correction automatically controls every outcome, exploratory comparison and calibration diagnostic jointly. Additional label repairs: final padded-stage ESS includes absorbed episodes; deterministic positive-weight task counts are 317–325, with 330 for the stochastic targets. The wrong-propensity negative control is a success estimate, and the −0.484 association compares reaching another decision with stopping, not switching versus retaining a model.
