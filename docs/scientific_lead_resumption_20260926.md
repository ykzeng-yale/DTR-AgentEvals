## Fixed research questions and current findings

The scientific question is whether randomized macro-model routing logs support trustworthy evaluation of
frozen dynamic policies, and whether observed history improves allocation under the declared common harness
and resources. The first question can succeed when no adaptive policy wins. An unfavorable result counts
toward completion if the study is adequate and its uncertainty is valid.
